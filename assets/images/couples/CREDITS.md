## What used to be here

Fourteen stock photographs from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
were downloaded for this folder early on and were never put on a page.
They were removed on 2026-11-06 because nothing referred to them and
they were making the repository 2.7 MB heavier for no reader. The
record is kept here so that the provenance of the set is not lost, and
so that a photographer who asks about one of them can be answered.

Under the Pexels License the photographs could be used for commercial
purposes without attribution. Attribution was given anyway while they
were on the site.

similar mood in the [Pexels couple collection](https://www.pexels.com/search/couple%20romance/), download with the same URL pattern, and update this table.
